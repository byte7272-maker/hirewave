Hirewave Connect

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 16, 2026

Hirewave Connect is a browser extension that lets you connect a job site (such as LinkedIn or Indeed) to your Hirewave account so Hirewave's assistant can act on that site on your behalf. This policy explains exactly what the extension accesses, what it sends, and what it never touches.

The short version: the extension captures your job-site session cookies — only when you click Connect, only for the site you choose — and sends them to Hirewave against a one-time pairing code. It never reads, stores, or transmits your password.

What the extension does

When you click Connect this site in the extension popup, it:

  • Reads the session cookies the job site already set in this browser after you logged in on that site's own page.
  • Packages those cookies into a session bundle.
  • Sends that bundle over HTTPS to the Hirewave API, matched to a short-lived, single-use pairing code you copied from the Hirewave app.

The extension takes no action on its own. It reads cookies only in direct response to your click, only for the job site you select, and only when you have supplied a valid pairing code.

What it accesses and why

  • Cookies (limited to the job-site domains in the manifest) — to capture the authenticated session so Hirewave can apply for you.
  • Active tab URL — only to auto-detect which supported job site you are on, so the correct provider is pre-selected. No browsing history is read or stored.
  • Local storage — to remember the Hirewave API address you set (defaults to the production Hirewave API). Stored only in this browser.

What it never accesses

  • Your passwords. The extension never reads, stores, or transmits any password or login credential.
  • Your Hirewave login token. The pairing code — not your Hirewave credentials — authorizes attaching the session.
  • Cookies or data from any site other than the supported job sites you explicitly connect.

What is sent, where, and how it is stored

  • Sent: the selected job site's session cookies + the pairing code + an optional label you type, to the Hirewave API over HTTPS.
  • Stored by Hirewave: the session is stored encrypted at rest and used solely to perform actions you request. It is scoped to your account.
  • Stored by the extension locally: only the Hirewave API address. No cookies or session data are retained in the extension after they are sent.

Data sharing

Hirewave does not sell your data and does not share captured sessions with third parties. Sessions are transmitted only to the Hirewave API.

Your control

  • The extension acts only when you click Connect.
  • Pairing codes are single-use and expire (about 10 minutes).
  • You can disconnect a site at any time from the Hirewave app, which invalidates the stored session.
  • Removing the extension stops all access immediately.

Contact

For questions about this policy or your data, contact the Hirewave team at the address published on the Hirewave website.
