# Bayete — Job-Search Automation Platform

**An end-to-end, AI-powered assistant that finds the right jobs, tailors your
application, checks postings for fraud, submits on your behalf, and even trains
you for the interview — with you in control at every step.**

Bayete streamlines the whole job hunt in one place. It connects to the services
you already use, understands your résumé and goals, and automates the repetitive
work of applying — while a mandatory review step ensures every submission is
authentically yours.

---

## What it does

**🔎 Smart job matching** — Ranks openings against your profile using AI semantic
matching (not just keywords), scoring each on skills, location, salary, and
seniority, and highlighting your matching skills and any gaps.

**🛡️ Authenticity checks** — Automatically screens every posting for fraud
signals (unrealistic promises, urgency red-flags, off-platform contact, implausible
pay) and hides scam listings before you ever see them.

**📝 Tailored documents** — Generates ATS-optimized résumés and cover letters
personalized to each job, or lets you **upload your own** (PDF/DOCX). Nothing is
submitted until you approve it.

**➤ Assisted applications** — Submits approved applications for you — via email
through your connected Gmail, or LinkedIn/Indeed quick-apply — with a manual
fallback if anything can't be completed automatically.

**🔗 Secure integrations** — Connects LinkedIn, Gmail, Google Drive, Indeed,
Greenhouse, and Workday through OAuth. You grant access, you can revoke it anytime,
and credentials are encrypted.

**🎤 Interview preparation** — A question bank with suggested answers grounded in
your résumé, plus a live **mock interview** with an AI interviewer persona: it
converses with you, can speak questions aloud and take spoken answers, rates each
answer on content and style (with response time and filler-word feedback), adapts
its difficulty with probing follow-ups, and saves your sessions to review.

**🔐 Security monitoring** — Checks whether your email has appeared in known data
breaches and whether a password has been exposed — and alerts you with steps to
fix it. Your data is monitored only with your consent, stored encrypted, and your
password never leaves your device.

**🔔 Notifications** — Real-time alerts for new matches, submitted applications,
required re-authentication, and security exposures.

---

## How it works

1. **Sign up** and build your profile (skills, experience, preferences).
2. **Connect** the accounts you want, and let Bayete pull in and rank jobs.
3. **Review matches** — scam postings are already filtered out.
4. **Generate or upload** a résumé and cover letter for a chosen role.
5. **Approve** your documents — the required human-in-the-loop gate.
6. **Submit** with one click; track status and get notified of the outcome.
7. **Prepare** for interviews and **monitor** your accounts for exposure.

---

## Built on trust

- **You're always in control** — nothing is sent to an employer without your
  explicit approval; no batch auto-submitting.
- **Authenticity first** — AI drafts are always surfaced for your review and edit.
- **Privacy by design** — data encrypted at rest, minimal retention, and you can
  disconnect or delete at any time.
- **Never overreaches** — CAPTCHAs are handed back to you (never bypassed), and
  the platform never fabricates answers or credentials on your behalf.
