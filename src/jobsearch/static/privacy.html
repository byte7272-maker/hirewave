Project Harbor Connect

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Project Harbor Connect is a browser extension that lets you connect a job site to your Project Harbor account, pull job listings you are viewing, and — when you ask it to — fill an application form with your own saved details. This policy explains exactly what the extension accesses, what it sends, and what it never touches.

The short version: the extension acts only when you ask it to, works with your own Project Harbor account, and never reads, stores, or transmits your password. It never submits an application for you — you review and submit.

What the extension does (always on your action)

  • Connect a site: when you choose to connect a job site you are signed in to, it reads only that site's existing session cookies (set after you logged in on that site's own page) and sends them to your Project Harbor account over HTTPS.
  • Pull jobs: on a job list you are viewing, it reads the listings on that page and sends them to your Project Harbor account so the assistant can match and tailor your resume.
  • Fill an application form: when you click “Fill with Harbor” on an application page, it reads the form's field labels and types (never password, hidden, or file fields, and never your existing answers), asks your Project Harbor account to map them to your own saved details, and fills those values into the form in your browser. It highlights what it filled; you review and submit.

The extension takes no action on its own — every action is a direct response to your click, on a supported job-site page.

Connecting the extension without copy-paste

To pull jobs or fill forms, the extension uses your own Project Harbor sign-in. When you click Enable in this browser in the Project Harbor app, the app hands the extension your current session token for your own account, used only to request short-lived access tokens. You never have to find, copy, or paste a token.

What it accesses and why

  • Cookies (limited to the job-site domains in the manifest) — to capture the authenticated session when you connect a site.
  • Active tab URL — only to auto-detect which supported job site you are on. No browsing history is read or stored.
  • Page content on supported job-site hosts — only the job listings you pull and the labels/types of an application form you choose to fill, only on your action. No other page content is read.
  • Local storage — the Project Harbor API address, and (if you enable pull/fill) your own Project Harbor session token, stored only in this browser to request short-lived access tokens for your account.

What it never accesses

  • Your passwords. The extension never reads, stores, or transmits any password or login credential — on a job site or in Project Harbor.
  • Password, hidden, or file fields on an application form, and your existing answers. It never submits a form.
  • Legal / eligibility answers. Work-authorization, sponsorship, veteran/disability, and consent questions are never auto-answered — they are left for you.
  • Cookies or data from any site other than the supported job sites you explicitly use.

What is sent, where, and how it is stored

  • Sent: connected sessions, pulled job listings, and application-form field labels — only to your own Project Harbor account over HTTPS, to perform the action you requested.
  • Stored by Project Harbor: connected sessions are stored encrypted at rest, scoped to your account, and used solely to perform actions you request.
  • Stored by the extension locally: the Project Harbor API address and your own session token (if you enabled pull/fill). Cookies and form content are not retained after they are sent.

Data sharing

Project Harbor does not sell your data and does not share your sessions, pulled jobs, or form data with third parties. They are transmitted only to your own Project Harbor account.

Your control

  • The extension acts only when you click Connect, Pull, or Fill.
  • You can disconnect a site at any time from the Project Harbor app, which invalidates the stored session.
  • Removing the extension stops all access immediately.

Contact

For questions about this policy or your data, contact the Project Harbor team at the address published on the Project Harbor website.
